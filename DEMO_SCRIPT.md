# Demo script (about 60 seconds)

**Before recording**
- Test in the exact spot you'll record in. Face a window or lamp.
- Close other tabs so the browser isn't lagging.
- Do one practice scan first so camera permission is already granted.

**0:00 - 0:10 | Hook**
"What if the phone already in your pocket could check your pulse?"

**0:10 - 0:35 | Live scan**
Show the app on the phone. Tap *Start scan*, look at the camera. The ring and progress bar run for 15 seconds. Speed this part up in editing if needed.

**0:35 - 0:45 | Result**
Show the BPM and the short message that appears.

**0:45 - 0:55 | Why it matters**
"Many people don't have easy access to regular health check-ins. VitalLens needs nothing but the camera they already own."

**0:55 - 1:00 | Close**
Team name, project name, thank you.

**Be ready for questions**
- *How accurate is it?* It's a wellness estimate. It works best in good light and when you stay still, and it tells you when the signal is too weak.
- *Is it medical?* No, and the app says so clearly.
- *Where does the data go?* Nowhere. It all runs on the device.
