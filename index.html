VitalLens

Look at your camera for 15 seconds. No touching the screen, no wearable, just your face.

For best results: face a light source, fill the frame with your face, and stay still.

VitalLens gives a wellness estimate using camera-based pulse detection (rPPG). It is not a medical device and does not diagnose any condition. If something feels off, please check with a doctor.
